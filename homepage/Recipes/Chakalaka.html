CHAKALAKA

Chakalaka is a spicy South African vegetable relish that is often served with bread, pap, or meat dishes. It is a flavorful and versatile dish that can be enjoyed in various ways.

Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 carrots, grated
  • 1 can (400g) baked beans in tomato sauce
  • 1 tablespoon paprika
  • 1-2 tablespoons curry powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the chopped onion and garlic, and sauté until they are soft and translucent.
  3. Add the chopped bell pepper and grated carrots to the pan. Cook for about 5-7 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  4. Stir in the baked beans along with their tomato sauce.
  5. Add the paprika, curry powder, salt, and pepper. Mix well to combine all the ingredients.
  6. Let the mixture simmer for about 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking.
  7. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. If you like it spicier, you can add more curry powder or a dash of sweet chilli sauce.
  8. Once cooked, remove from heat and let it cool slightly before serving.

Serve the chakalaka as a side dish with bread, pap, or grilled meats. Garnish with fresh coriander if desired. Enjoy your chakalaka!
