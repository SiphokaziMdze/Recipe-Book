UMQA

Umqa is a traditional Xhosa dish originating from the Eastern Cape province of South Africa, consisting of a stiff porridge made from pumpkin (or squash) and maize meal.

It is a beloved comfort food, often described as a thick, mashed pumpkin pap that is seasoned with sugar and salt.

Ingredients:

  • 1 medium-sized pumpkin (about 2-3 kg)
  • 2 cups maize meal (cornmeal)
  • 4 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons sugar (optional, adjust to taste)

Instructions:

  1. Start by preparing the pumpkin. Cut it in half, remove the seeds, and peel off the skin. Then, cut the pumpkin flesh into small cubes.
  2. In a large pot, bring 4 cups of water to a boil. Add the cubed pumpkin to the boiling water and cook until the pumpkin is soft and tender (about 15-20 minutes).
  3. Once the pumpkin is cooked, use a potato masher or fork to mash it until smooth. You can also use a blender for a smoother consistency.
  4. In a separate bowl, mix the maize meal with a little cold water to create a smooth paste. This helps prevent lumps when adding it to the hot pumpkin mixture.
  5. Gradually add the maize meal paste to the mashed pumpkin in the pot, stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
  6. Add salt and sugar (if using) to taste. Continue to cook over low heat, stirring frequently, until the mixture thickens and reaches your desired consistency (about 10-15 minutes).
  7. Once cooked, remove from heat and let it cool slightly before serving.

Umqa is typically served as a side dish alongside meat, vegetables, or stews. Enjoy your umqa!
