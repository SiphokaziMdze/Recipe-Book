PEPPERMINT CRISP TART

This classic South African dessert is a delightful combination of layers of tennis biscuits, caramel treat, whipped cream, and peppermint crisp chocolate. It's a no-bake dessert that's easy to prepare and perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients:

  • 1 packet of tennis biscuits (or any plain tea biscuits)
  • 1 can of caramel treat (dulce de leche)
  • 500 ml of whipped cream
  • 1 bar of peppermint crisp chocolate, grated

Instructions:

  1. In a rectangular dish, arrange a layer of tennis biscuits to cover the bottom.
  2. Spread a generous layer of caramel treat over the biscuits.
  3. Sprinkle a layer of grated peppermint crisp chocolate over the caramel treat.
  4. Repeat the layers until you have used all the ingredients, finishing with a layer of whipped cream on top.
  5. Sprinkle the remaining grated peppermint crisp chocolate on top of the whipped cream for garnish.
  6. Refrigerate the tart for at least 4 hours, or overnight, to allow it to set and for the flavors to meld together.
