UMVUBO

This is a delicious South African meal mostly enjoyed by Xhosa people. Umvubo is a very simple and quick meal we enjoy in the summer/warm months.

It is made from Umphokoqo and Amasi

Now, lets make Umphokoqo:

Ingredients:

  • 3 cup of maize meal
  • 2 cups of water
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt

Instructions:

  1. In a pot, bring the water to a boil.
  2. Add the salt to the boiling water.
  3. Gradually add the maize meal while stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
  4. Ensure that you add more maize meal than water, as we need umphokoqo to look like scrambled eggs.
  5. Reduce the heat to low and cover the pot. Let it simmer for about 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally until it is scrambled and is cooked through.
  6. Once cooked, remove from heat and let it cool slightly before serving.

To serve, scoop some umphokoqo onto a plate and pour amasi over it. Enjoy your umvubo!

Note: Amasi is a traditional South African fermented milk product, similar to buttermilk. You can find it in most South African grocery stores.

Umphokoqo can also be enjoyed with fresh milk.
