ISIDUDU

Isidudu is a traditional South African porridge made from maize meal. It is a popular breakfast dish that is both hearty and nutritious.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup maize meal (cornmeal)
  • 4 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Sugar or honey to taste
  • Milk, cream, or vinegar(my personal fav)

Instructions:

  1. In a medium-sized pot, bring the water to a boil.
  2. Add the salt to the boiling water.
  3. Gradually add the maize meal while stirring continuously to prevent lumps from forming.
  4. Reduce the heat to low and continue to cook, stirring frequently, until the mixture thickens and the maize meal is cooked through (about 15-20 minutes).
  5. Once cooked, remove from heat and let it cool slightly before serving.
  6. Serve the isidudu hot, sweetened with sugar or honey, and topped with milk, cream, or a splash of vinegar according to your preference.
